A single 1024×1024 image summing up Wednesday 25 November 2026's world news. Built for the [news-image-20261125 bounty on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125).

1. **Ukraine confirms ship strikes** — President Zelenskyy confirmed Ukraine hit a Russian cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland and sank a warship off Benghazi, Libya.
2. **CIA and ODNI hacked** — the CIA and the Office of the Director of National Intelligence were hit by a hack of an unclassified open-source intelligence system; no classified data was affected.
3. **Riyadh tower hit again** — a Houthi drone strike hit Riyadh's tallest tower, the PIF headquarters, wounding 11 people, the second hit in less than a week.
4. **Iran claims tankers hit mines** — Iran's Revolutionary Guard said three oil tankers hit naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz under US escort; the claim is unverified.
5. **Ebola staff safe in Congo** — WHO evacuated staff from a hospital in Akwe, DR Congo, after they sheltered there for three days amid fierce fighting; the Ebola team was relocated from Mongbwalu.
6. **Huawei found guilty** — a US jury found Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 counts, including stealing trade secrets and bank fraud.

## How to run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `out/entry.png`. Node 22, standard library only, no network.

The image itself was drawn by `news-image-20261125-draw.py` (pure Python standard library, no third-party code) and is shipped in this repository.
